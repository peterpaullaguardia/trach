# TRACH

An AI work assistant that keeps work organized and makes sure instructions actually get followed.

**Status:** in development. Used to coach and track hours across my own VA team.

## The problem

SOPs and video walkthroughs get skimmed, then people guess, and billable hours are hard to report exactly.

## What it does

**SOP coaching**

- Takes a document, procedure or video walkthrough and simplifies it
- Asks whether you got everything, then tests your understanding
- Grades on whether you got the concept, not exact wording
- Lists anything unclear in the source so it can be raised before work starts

**Time tracking**

- Tracks time per task and pauses automatically on breaks
- Counts only work on the platforms that matter for the job
- Rolls hours into a billable timesheet and a team dashboard

**Assistant**

- A voice assistant that appears when called, with SOP coaching and hours in one app
